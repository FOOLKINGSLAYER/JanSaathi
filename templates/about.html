Why JanSaathi

Make the distance between people and solutions smaller.

JanSaathi is a prototype collaboration portal for community-led innovation. It helps a real problem travel clearly from local experience to research, partnership, testing and impact.

PEOPLE · IDEAS · SOLUTIONS

Why this matters

A good idea should not lose momentum between the first conversation and the first prototype.

Communities often know what is failing long before a formal project exists. Universities may have the relevant knowledge, students and facilities. Industry may have the tools to test and scale. Government may hold the context needed for implementation.

JanSaathi gives those perspectives a shared record and a visible next step without presenting AI recommendations or prototype data as official decisions.
