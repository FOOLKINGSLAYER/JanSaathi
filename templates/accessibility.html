Prototype content · reviewed 05 September 2026

Our approach

JanSaathi has been designed with WCAG 2.1 Level AA and applicable GIGW 3.0 accessibility principles in mind. This prototype is not formally certified.

Implemented in this prototype

  • Semantic page landmarks, logical headings and a skip-to-content link.
  • Visible labels, focus states and understandable form feedback.
  • Responsive layouts intended to remain usable at increased zoom.
  • Text labels alongside status colours and chart context.
  • Reduced-motion support for non-essential movement.

Known limitations

Language translations, full screen-reader chart alternatives, production accessibility testing and external document accessibility remain future work before deployment.
